# 🏠 SaathiGhar

### Because Every Parent Deserves to Be Remembered.

SaathiGhar is a digital elderly-care platform designed to connect elderly residents with their families while helping ashrams manage residents, communication, care, and community support through a unified platform.

The platform brings together **family connection, elderly care, ashram management, volunteer participation, donations, and AI assistance** into one ecosystem.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

### 👨‍👩‍👧 Family Portal
A dedicated portal that helps families stay connected with elderly residents.

- Resident information and updates
- Family communication
- Photos and memories
- Notifications and important updates
- Support and sponsorship features

### 👴 Elderly Resident Portal
A simplified interface designed specifically for elderly residents.

- Large, accessible controls
- Family communication
- Photo and memory access
- Simple navigation
- Resident-friendly interactions

### 🏢 Ashram Portal
A management interface for participating ashrams.

- Resident management
- Family connections
- Resident profiles
- Care-related information
- Notifications and communication
- Ashram management tools

### 🤝 Volunteer Portal
A dedicated experience for volunteers who want to contribute to elderly care.

- Volunteer participation
- Support opportunities
- Community involvement
- Interaction with participating ashrams
- Volunteer-related activities and coordination

### 🏠 Find Your Ashram
A discovery system that helps families find suitable ashrams.

- Ashram search
- Location-based discovery
- Map-based interface
- Ashram information
- Verification-focused discovery experience

### 💝 Donations & Sponsorship
A donation system designed to make supporting elderly care simple and transparent.

- Predefined donation amounts
- Custom donation amounts
- Sponsorship options
- Anonymous donations
- Personal donor messages
- Online payment integration

### 🤖 Dadi AI
An AI companion concept designed specifically around elderly users.

The goal is to provide a more natural, approachable conversational experience rather than a generic chatbot, making technology easier and more comfortable for elderly residents to interact with.
