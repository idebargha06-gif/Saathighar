### 🏠 SaathiGhar

### Because Every Parent Deserves to Be Remembered.

SaathiGhar is a digital elderly-care and family accountability platform designed to connect families with elderly residents living in ashrams.

The platform brings together **family connection, verified ashrams, elderly care, donations, sponsorships, and digital communication** into one ecosystem — helping families stay connected with their parents while giving ashrams better tools to manage residents and communicate with families.

> **One check-in at a time. Never let your parent feel forgotten.**

---

## 🌟 What is SaathiGhar?

Many families want to ensure that their elderly parents living in ashrams are safe, cared for, and emotionally connected — but distance, lack of information, and limited communication systems can make this difficult.

SaathiGhar aims to solve this by creating a platform where:

- 👨‍👩‍👧 Families can stay connected with elderly parents
- 🏠 Families can discover and find verified ashrams
- 👴 Elderly residents can access a simplified digital interface
- 🏢 Ashram administrators can manage residents and family connections
- 💝 Donors can sponsor elderly residents and contribute to their care
- 🤖 AI assistance can provide a more accessible conversational experience
- 💳 Online payments can support donations and platform services

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 👨‍👩‍👧 Family Portal

A dedicated experience for family members to:

- Stay connected with elderly parents
- Check resident information
- View updates and photos
- Access family communication features
- Support and sponsor elderly care

### 🏠 Find an Ashram

A location-based discovery experience that allows families to:

- Search for ashrams
- Find nearby care facilities
- View available ashram information
- Explore verified care options
- Filter/search based on location and requirements

### 👴 Elderly Resident Interface

A simplified interface designed specifically for elderly residents.

The interface focuses on:

- Large, easy-to-understand controls
- Family calling
- Photo viewing
- Simple navigation
- Accessible interactions
- Minimal cognitive complexity

### 🤖 Dadi AI

SaathiGhar includes an AI companion concept designed around elderly users.

Rather than treating the AI as a generic chatbot, the goal is to provide a more natural and approachable conversational experience that can help residents interact with technology more comfortably.

### 💝 Donations & Sponsorship

The platform provides a donation experience where users can:

- Select predefined donation amounts
- Enter a custom donation amount
- Sponsor specific aspects of elderly care
- Donate anonymously
- Add a personal message
- Make online payments

### 🏢 Ashram Portal

A dedicated management experience for participating ashrams.

The platform supports concepts such as:

- Resident management
- Family connections
- Manager accounts
- Resident profiles
- Care-related information
- Communication and monitoring

### 💳 Payment Integration

Online payment functionality is incorporated for:

- Donations
- Sponsorships
- Platform subscriptions/services

### 📱 Responsive Design

The interface is designed for:

- Desktop
- Tablet
- Mobile devices

Mobile-specific layouts are used where simply shrinking the desktop interface would negatively affect usability.
