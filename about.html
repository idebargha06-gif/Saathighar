Why We Founded SaathiGhar

In India, over 1.5 million elderly citizens live in senior care homes and ashrams. While many facilities provide food and shelter, the deepest ache seniors face is loneliness — feeling disconnected from children working in distant cities or overseas.

SaathiGhar was created to solve this emotional gap. We built a digital ecosystem that makes family check-ins effortless, holds children accountable with gentle reminders, and empowers ashram staff to digitize resident healthcare records.

"A phone call from a child is better than any prescription medicine for an elderly parent."

— SaathiGhar Founding Philosophy

Our Core Values

The principles that guide every decision, feature, and partnership at SaathiGhar.

Value 01

Compassion First

Every feature is designed with elder-friendly accessibility — large fonts, voice controls, and staff assistance so technology never becomes a barrier.

Value 02

Radical Transparency

100% of donor funds reach partner ashram care accounts directly. We provide digitized delivery receipts and photo verification for every sponsorship.

Value 03

Family Accountability

We nudge adult children gently without shame, encouraging regular connection and financial responsibility for their aging parents.
