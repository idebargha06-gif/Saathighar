Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about your donation? Find answers below.

Is my donation tax-deductible under 80G? ▼
Yes! All donations made on SaathiGhar are eligible for 50% tax exemption under Section 80G of the Indian Income Tax Act. Your 80G tax receipt will be sent directly to your email.
How do I know my donation actually reached the elder? ▼
We provide 100% transparent tracking. You receive photo updates and digitized receipts from the partner ashram showing the delivery of meals or medicines.
Can I donate anonymously? ▼
Absolutely. Simply check the "Donate anonymously" option on the form. Your personal identity will be kept strictly private and hidden from the public wall.
