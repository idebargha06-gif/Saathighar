📜 Donor Help & Tax Guidelines

Donation Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers regarding 80G tax exemption receipts, payment security, direct elder impact, and anonymity options.

📜 80G Tax Exemption & Receipts
Is my donation eligible for 80G tax exemption? ▼
Yes, all monetary contributions made through SaathiGhar to registered partner ashrams and elderly welfare programs are eligible for up to 50% tax exemption under Section 80G of the Indian Income Tax Act.
How and when will I receive my 80G tax receipt? ▼
As soon as your payment is processed via Razorpay, a digital 80G receipt (with PAN details and 80G Registration Number) is automatically generated and sent to your registered email address within minutes.
Is PAN card mandatory for claiming 80G benefits? ▼
As per Indian Income Tax regulations, providing your PAN number is required if you wish to receive an official 80G tax receipt for tax filing purposes. If you do not require a tax receipt, PAN is optional.
🔒 Payments, Anonymity & Security
Are online payments safe on SaathiGhar? ▼
Yes! All online transactions are processed through Razorpay, India's leading PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant payment gateway. We support UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), NetBanking, Credit/Debit Cards, and Wallets. SaathiGhar does not store your card or bank details.
Can I donate anonymously? ▼
Yes. You can simply check the "Donate anonymously" box on the donation checkout form. Your name will be hidden from the public Wall of Kindness and from public donor lists.
🤝 Direct Impact & Transparency
How does my money reach the elderly resident? ▼
98% of resident sponsorships are directly remitted to the verified ashram care manager's bank account for purchasing meals, medicines, or personal care supplies. A tiny 2% platform fee covers payment processing fees.
Will I get updates about the resident I sponsored? ▼
Yes! For resident sponsorships, donors receive periodic photo updates and gratitude notes from the ashram staff showcasing how your contribution provided meals, medicines, or comfort.

Have additional questions or need assistance?
