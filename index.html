Simple, Powerful Steps

How SaathiGhar Works

A four-step system that turns good intentions into consistent, accountable care.

📋
Register Your Parent
Add your parent's profile and link their ashram. Takes under 5 minutes.
🤝
Commit to a Care Plan
Set your goal — weekly calls, monthly visits, timely payments. Your promise, your pace.
🔔
Get Gentle Reminders
We nudge you before check-ins are due — SMS, email, or app. No more forgetting.
✅
Ashram Confirms Care
Your ashram verifies each interaction. Full accountability, both ways, always.
