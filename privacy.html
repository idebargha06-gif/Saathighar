🔒 Privacy & Data Protection

Privacy Policy

We are dedicated to safeguarding the privacy, dignity, and security of our elderly residents, families, donors, and ashram partners.

Last Updated: August 2026 • Compliant with DPDP Act 2023

1. Introduction & Core Commitment

SaathiGhar ("we", "our", or "us") operates the SaathiGhar platform connecting elderly residents in old-age homes across India with their family members, compassionate donors, volunteers, and ashram managers. Respecting dignity and data privacy is central to our mission.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, process, and protect your personal information when you use our website, mobile interface, resident tablets, and digital donation tools, in full compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 and the Indian Information Technology Act, 2000.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on your interaction with SaathiGhar, we collect the following categories of information:

  • Family Members & Users: Full name, email address, mobile number, relationship to the resident, account login credentials, and communication logs.
  • Elderly Residents: Name, age, resident photo, medical care updates entered by verified staff, dietary preferences, and staff-assisted video call records.
  • Donors: Name, email address, transaction history, optional personal message, and PAN card number (required exclusively for generating Section 80G tax exemption receipts).
  • Ashram Managers & Staff: Official ashram registration documents, manager contact details, capacity stats, and bank payout details.
🛡️ Zero Financial Data Storage: SaathiGhar does NOT store or process credit/debit card numbers, UPI PINs, or banking passwords. All online payments are handled directly by PCI-DSS Level 1 certified gateways (Razorpay).

3. How We Use Your Information

We process personal data strictly for legitimate operational purposes:

  • Enabling family video calls and real-time resident care updates.
  • Facilitating direct sponsorships and issuing instant 80G tax deduction receipts.
  • Operating the 24/7 SOS Emergency Alert System for elderly residents.
  • Improving Dadi AI voice companion responses for conversational comfort.
  • Verifying old-age homes to prevent fraud and ensure resident safety.

4. Data Sharing & Disclosure Policy

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade personal data to any third party or marketing agency under any circumstances. Data is shared only under strict conditions:

  • Verified Ashram Staff: Care updates are shared strictly between authorized ashram personnel and registered family members.
  • Legal & Emergency Helpline Desk: In life-threatening emergencies, relevant details may be shared with emergency services (112, 108, 14567 Elder Line).
  • Government Compliance: As required by applicable law, court orders, or statutory authorities.

5. Donor Anonymity Options

Donors have the full right to contribute anonymously. When you check the "Donate anonymously" box, your name and identity will be omitted from public donor walls, news feeds, and recipient-facing lists.

6. Security Measures & Data Retention

We employ 256-bit SSL encryption for data in transit and Row-Level Security (RLS) policies powered by Supabase for data at rest. Access to sensitive resident records is restricted solely to verified staff accounts with multi-factor authentication.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Under the DPDP Act 2023, you have the right to:

  • Access, review, or request a copy of your personal data.
  • Request correction or updating of inaccurate data.
  • Withdraw consent or request complete deletion of your account.
