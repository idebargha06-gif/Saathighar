2. Platform Services & User Portals

SaathiGhar provides digital bridge tools connecting senior citizen homes across India with family members, volunteers, donors, and healthcare managers:

  • Family Members: Granted secure access to track resident wellbeing, view photo updates, schedule staff-assisted calls, and contribute to care expenses.
  • Ashram Managers: Agree to maintain accurate resident rosters, submit truthful care reports, maintain facility hygiene standards, and utilize donor funds exclusively for elder welfare.

3. Donations, Fees & 80G Tax Certificates

Monetary contributions made on SaathiGhar directly support resident care (meals, medicines, healthcare supplies) or ashram infrastructure:
